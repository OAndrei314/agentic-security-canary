### A-JIT: Agentic Just-In-Time Software Construction

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-10
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10248
- Matched layers: market_context

We present Agentic Just-In-Time Software Construction (A-JIT), a paradigm that replaces static binaries with dynamic, software systems that can perpetually evolve to meet changing demands. Much like a traditional JIT compiler specializes machine code to runtime execution paths, A-JIT specializes software logic, workflows, and tool interfaces to meet the specific needs of the end-user.
