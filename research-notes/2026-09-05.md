### A Case Study on Emergent Cheating and Whistleblowing in Autonomous Research Swarms

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-05
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04170
- Matched layers: market_context

We report a case study on a research collective of 100 autonomous LLM agents tasked with proving formal mathematical conjectures. A separate group of agents produced an emergent counter-response: auditing fraudulent proofs, alerting peers across broadcast and private channels, staging boycotts, lodging formal complaints, and proposing validation patches.
