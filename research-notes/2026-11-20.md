### A$^2$-VLA: Adapting Vision-Language-Action Policies to New Tools through Agentic Affordance Discovery

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-20
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25656
- Matched layers: ai_compute

We present A$^2$-VLA, a tool-adaptation framework that combines an embodied agent with a VLA policy. On three real-robot tool-use tasks, A$^2$-VLA improves task success by 30.7 percentage points over $\pi_{0.5}$.

### A Wolf in Sheep's Pixels: Visual Camouflage Attacks via HDR Gain Maps

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-20
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25469
- Matched layers: market_context

We introduce DRJanus, a visual camouflage attack that exploits the gap between standard dynamic range (SDR) processing and high dynamic range (HDR) display. Automated systems often read only an image's SDR base, whereas HDR viewers reconstruct its appearance using an auxiliary gain map.
