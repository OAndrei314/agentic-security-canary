### A Good Self-Teacher Meets the Student Where They Are: Joint On-Policy Learning and Teaching

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-08
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10447
- Matched layers: ai_compute

On-Policy Distillation (OPD) offers an attractive alternative by providing dense token-level supervision from a stronger teacher along the student's own generations. Based on this result, we propose Joint On-Policy Learning and Teaching (JOLT), which jointly trains a single policy in two roles: a privileged teacher using a KL-regularized objective, and an unprivileged student using dense on-policy distillation.
