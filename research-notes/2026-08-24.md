### $Z^2$-ACT: End-to-End Verifiable Agentic Intent Control for Open 6G RAN

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-24
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21049
- Matched layers: market_context

In order to host multi-vendors, it is essential that AI-assisted control loops remain safe, verifiable, and auditable under concurrent operator intents and untrusted model inputs. In this regard, we propose zero-knowledge auditable control and zero-trust verifiable agentic intent architecture ($Z^2$-ACT), which integrates the aforementioned four primitives across the non-real-time and near-real-time RICs.

### AEGIS: Preventing Cross-Domain Resource Abuse in MCP

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-24
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20481
- Matched layers: market_context

Each modality including text, images, video, and location introduces distinct vectors for resource abuse, complicating the development of consistent mitigation strategies. In this paper, we present AEGIS, a policy enforcement component that enables administrators to define fine-grained safeguards against resource abuse across heterogeneous MCP tools and modalities.

### AgentOCR: Reimagining Agent History via Optical Self-Compression

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-24
