### A False Average: Pooled CoT-Monitor Accuracy Conceals a Reasoning-Dependent Fragility

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-17
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583
- Matched layers: market_context

On Terminal Wrench, about 77% of hacks are given away by the actions alone, and the monitor's pooled accuracy is dominated by them; on the remaining 23%, which an action-only monitor misses, the same monitor is fragile. Monitor accuracy should be reported on the reasoning-dependent subset, where the defense is load-bearing.

### A Hierarchical Statistical Framework for Evaluating Agent Reliability

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-17
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19614
- Matched layers: market_context

This paper presents a statistical framework for evaluating uncertainty and reliability in agentic systems. Aggregating metrics at different levels of the hierarchy reveals different structures and properties of the policy model; we connect this hierarchical aggregation with the known process of \emph{coarse-graining} as intended in the physics and statistical literature.
